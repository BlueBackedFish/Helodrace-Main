# Telegraph network checks

Run `dotnet run --project Source/Tests/TelegraphNetwork/TelegraphNetwork.Tests.csproj` from the repository root.

Run `Source/Tests/TelegraphNetwork/Verify-MenuProvider.ps1` in Windows PowerShell to compare the built provider's actual menu eligibility flags against installed RimWorld's vanilla trade provider. This checks drafted/undrafted selection without starting Unity.

The tests compile the production connection rules and map component against minimal game stubs. They cover payment from accessible notes, insufficient funds, repeated requests, LOW faction restrictions, independent settlement connections, installation deadlines, persisted provider/deadline/notification state, and opening-hour boundaries. The stubs exercise `ExposeData`; they do not replace an in-game save/load check.

The same suite compiles the production financial-goodwill and loan components. It checks gift/trade half rewards, paid-service rewards, fractional balances and rounding, financial caps, contract eligibility and collateral, duplicate requests, scoped financial-caravan delivery, partial physical placement, cancelled deliveries, partial/early/overdue repayments, unavailable money, independent diplomatic goodwill, and save/load throughout the contract lifecycle. Native cash placement, caravan movement, and document rendering still require play checks. `Communications/Verify-SafeConductHooks.ps1` also verifies the actual installed game's loan-delivery meeting state and destination field.

In-game checks still required:

1. Build and fuel a telegraph. Verify use is unavailable before installation and the inspect text explains how to connect.
2. Select a colonist and right-click a visiting LOW caravan trader, both drafted and undrafted. Verify ordinary trading and the separate installation request both appear. Clicking the same caravan's escort must also offer installation and send the colonist to its merchant. HIGH traders and unrelated LOW visitors must not offer installation. A merchant's temporarily unavailable merchandise trade must not hide installation.
3. Request installation, cancel once, then confirm with 500 accessible Sthaler. Verify only confirmation charges the fee and repeating the request is unavailable.
4. Save/load during the 2–3 day wait. Verify completion time persists, the activation letter appears once, and another telegraph in the same settlement shares the connection.
5. At 08:00–17:59 local time, expand the LOW faction row and company rows. At 18:00–07:59, or without primary cells, verify all company names and buttons disappear.
6. Test the separate financial and commercial company requests, the combined request, and Harrington guard hiring as described in `Source/Tests/Communications/README.md`.
7. Verify contracts and support show only the contracted LOW faction. Recheck a support target after the relay closes; the request must fail without spending service capacity or ammunition. SCR-300 calls must retain their existing behavior.
8. Verify installation, Harrington hiring and successful forward-base period settlement award one financial goodwill per 20 Sthaler actually paid. Failed or empty payments must not award anything.
9. Open the National Industrial Credit Corporation loan contract. At diplomatic 30 / financial 40, sign for 800 Sthaler over five days. Verify the 40-point deduction and one cell, no immediate money, the arriving financial caravan walking into the colony and dropping 800 cash, and the five-day term beginning then. Save/reload while pending, walking, and after delivery. Test rejection below combined 70 or above collateral. Try the same agreement from another settlement to rule out duplicate borrowing.
10. Repay 201 and then 599 from accessible notes. Verify credit restoration of 10.05 and 29.95, no service bonus, and no duplicate repayment. Let a loan become overdue and check its single notice, new-loan restriction, and release after full repayment. Check a dead/departed courier or removed destination returns only the undelivered collateral. Verify the contract paper, fields, long translated terms, and signing/repayment controls remain readable with scrolling on a small screen.
11. Default on a loan and a forward-base bill. Each must record its unpaid Sthaler converted to silver in the matching loss category exactly once, including after save/load. Loan default deducts 30 diplomatic goodwill and half the collateral used by that funded loan, independent of current finance or partial repayments. Forward-base default deducts exactly 15 diplomatic and 30 financial goodwill, replacing its former penalties. Test zero/negative/current balances, partially cancelled deliveries, and the -100 floor. Repaying overdue principal restores only the loan collateral, keeps the additional penalty and historical arrears entry, and cannot bypass default processing between 250-tick checks.
