# Batch still

Original 3×3 fired copper batch kettle, broad conical head, overhead vapor pipe and separate water-cooled condenser. A small receiver and two cooling-water ports explain the flow at sprite scale. Stone is tintable; copper and iron keep their fixed colors. The original texture is preserved in `Reference/` and packed in the Blender scene.

`build_still.py` exports the editable scene and 768px North/East/South/West color/mask pairs. All views use 64° downward projection, shared tile density and a fixed 12px outline. Existing recipes, wood fuel, construction costs and research remain unchanged. No in-game verification has been performed.

Process guidance: [Penn State, historical batch distillation](https://courses.ems.psu.edu/fsc432/node/755). The design simplifies the heated kettle, vapor line and condenser into a related game machine rather than reproducing a historical still.
