# 1970s hydraulic four-high plate mill

Original 4x3 circa-1972-1979 electric plate mill. Two work rolls and two larger backup rolls sit between heavy housings; four prominent feed rollers, a broad plate, hydraulic gap actuators, reducer/motor and separate control/hydraulic units give it a different silhouette from the original simple rolling machine. The footprint is a compact sprite abstraction of a much larger production system.

`build_mill.py` exports `HD_RollingMill1970.blend`, four actual 64-degree orthographic views, 1024px color/mask pairs and fixed 12px outlines. drawSize is (6,6), with the compensated ground-anchor offset in `validation.json`. No lighting, AO or view-dependent colors are used.

The active staffed worktable uses 6000W and work-speed factor 3, retains uniform-steel-plate production and unlocks with industrial automation. Shaft-network requirements are replaced by electricity.

Period guidance: [JFE/ISIJ history of thick-plate rolling technology](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/isijinternational/55/1/55_67/_html/-char/ja) documents process control in the early 1970s and hydraulic gauge-control adoption later in that decade. This is a new simplified machine variant, not a scale reconstruction of a particular mill. No in-game visual/play test has been performed.
