# Blast furnace

Original 4×4 compact hot-blast furnace with a substantial stone hearth, tapered iron shaft, charging bell, binding bands, two small hot-blast stoves, bustle main and front tapping runner. A coarse ladder and gas downcomer make the side/back views useful without miniature engineering detail. Stone stays fixed; iron uses the green secondary mask with vanilla Steel gray. The charging bell and stove crowns now use subdued metal tones.

`build_furnace.py` exports an editable scene and 1024px North/East/South/West color/mask pairs: 64° downward, fixed 12px outline, shared tile density. Existing fuel, recipes, work speed and converter facility link are preserved. References are packed in the scene. No in-game verification has been performed.

Structural guidance: [ASME, Nassawango Iron Furnace](https://www.asme.org/about-asme/engineering-history/landmarks/159-nassawango-iron-furnace), and [Library of Congress, blast-furnace study](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/master/pnp/habshaer/pa/pa4100/pa4149/data/pa4149data.pdf). This is a simplified related machine, not a historical-site reconstruction.
