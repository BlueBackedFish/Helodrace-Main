# Four-direction industrial assets

All newly authored furnaces and the cable-tool rig now have saved North / East / South / West orthographic cameras at 64° downward, with real West renders. Existing nonrotatable models were exported from their saved meshes without changing geometry or painted colors. Their existing N/E/S PNG bytes remain unchanged. `verify_rotation.py` compares them with commit `e5af09e3` and checks saved mesh/material hashes, camera directions, margins, XML texture links, color/mask alpha and interaction-cell placement.

New assets: arc furnace (`ElectricArcFurnace`, 4×4, drawSize 6.5), vacuum induction furnace (`VacuumInductionFurnace`, 3×4, drawSize 6), and cable-tool rig (`CableToolRig`, 4×3, drawSize 6.5). All use zero drawOffset, the established neutral industrial palette, 12px exterior outlines, and fixed Emission shading. Build each with its own script, then run `integrate_asset.py <DefName> <source-folder>` with ordinary Python to synchronize its presentation fields.

Existing definitions enabled for rotation: small/large steam engines, industrial diesel engine, generator, motor, high-pressure reactor, stirred-tank reactor, wooden pumpjack and distillation tower. The reactors use `Graphic_Multi` with unsuffixed texPaths. Their existing footprint, costs, research, recipes, power, fuel, heat and production remain unchanged. Steel pumpjack and rotary drill also have real West artwork; they remain art-only assets with no invented gameplay definitions. Buildings that already rotated, and unmodeled buildings, retain their existing settings.

Radio correction is recorded separately in `CollinsRadioDesk/direction_correction.json`: the active North/South PNG bytes were exchanged exactly. Saved camera names and the builder now agree: North shows controls, South shows cabinet backs. The radio has a real West render at its existing 256px size and no color mask.

Engine smoke now uses the saved 3D stack outlet, rotates its ground coordinates, then projects its height at 64° so smoke stays above the stack in every view. It is anchored to `DrawPos`, the same center as the building sprite. Small-steam and diesel outlet positions are checked against actual mesh vertices. The large compound engine has no combustion stack, so its obsolete smoke comp was removed. No production/fuel behavior was changed. `Source/Tests/IndustrialSmoke` exercises all four projections and the stopped-engine condition.

Run Blender with `--python ArtSource/IndustrialMachines/verify_rotation.py`, `--python ArtSource/IndustrialMachines/verify_assets.py`, and `--python ArtSource/PetroleumMachines/verify_petroleum.py`. The combined checks cover 15 saved scenes, 60 camera views and 116 PNGs. `review_rotation.py` creates review-only sheets on gray backgrounds; the game images stay transparent. No in-game visual/play test has been performed.
