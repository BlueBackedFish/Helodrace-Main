# Circa-1950 rotary drilling rig

Original steel land rig, active as `HD_RotaryDrill`: 4x4 footprint, 1024px North/East/South/West color/mask pairs, drawSize (6,6), zero drawOffset, 64-degree downward orthographic cameras and fixed 12px outline. Four braced derrick legs surround a rotary table, square Kelly and real floor opening. Crown sheaves, travelling block, drawworks, engine enclosure, mud tank/pump, standpipe and three-pipe rack establish the period machinery without dense internals.

Gameplay is in `Defs/WildWest/Buildings/AdvancedOilExtraction.xml`: diesel capacity 40, consumption 20/day only while actively drilling, guaranteed completion after 3 drilling days, possible earlier strike and colonist maintenance. Developed well quality ranges 1-1.5. Research: high oil development. A completed rig stops using fuel and can be dismantled to place a pump over its field. The maintenance workgiver scans all oil-development buildings. English and Korean labels/descriptions are supplied.

Build in Blender with `--python ArtSource/RotaryDrill/build_drill.py`, then publish using `--python ArtSource/IndustrialMachines/normalize_density.py -- HD_RotaryDrill`. Scene: `HD_RotaryDrill.blend`; prefix: `Buildings/HD_RotaryDrill`. See `validation.json` and `preview_four_views.png`. Source geometry and painted colors were retained when enabling the building. No in-game visual/play test has been performed.

Period guidance: [ASME's Mr. Charlie brochure](https://dev12.asme.org/wwwasmeorg/media/resourcefiles/aboutasme/who%20we%20are/engineering%20history/landmarks/250-mr-charlie-oil-drilling-rig.pdf) and [Alberta's rotary-drilling history](https://history.alberta.ca/energyheritage/gas/local-markets-only/an-incidental-nuisance-product/rotary-tool-drill.aspx). Only the equipment family is borrowed; this is a compact original land skid.
