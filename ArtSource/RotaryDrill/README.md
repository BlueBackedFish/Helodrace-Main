# Circa-1950 rotary drilling rig

New original land-rig artwork, proposed footprint 4×4. Four tapering steel derrick legs and three large braced bays surround a rotary table and square Kelly. Crown sheaves, travelling block/swivel, drawworks and separate engine enclosure identify a mid-century rotary rig. One mud tank, reciprocating pump, standpipe/Kelly hose and three-pipe rack explain the circulation and pipe-handling equipment. A real opening in the raised deck sits below the rotary-table ring. No modern top drive, hydraulic telescoping mast, touchscreen or forest of tiny hoses is added.

Historical guidance: [ASME's Mr. Charlie landmark brochure](https://dev12.asme.org/wwwasmeorg/media/resourcefiles/aboutasme/who%20we%20are/engineering%20history/landmarks/250-mr-charlie-oil-drilling-rig.pdf) documents a 1952–1953 rig with rotary-table, hoisting and power equipment. Only the period equipment family is borrowed; this model is a compact land skid, not an offshore barge reconstruction. [Alberta's government rotary-drilling history](https://history.alberta.ca/energyheritage/gas/local-markets-only/an-incidental-nuisance-product/rotary-tool-drill.aspx) describes the power, hoisting, rotating and circulation systems used as the model's functional groups.

Build in Blender background mode with `--python ArtSource/RotaryDrill/build_drill.py`. Scene `HD_RotaryDrill.blend`; prefix `Buildings/HD_RotaryDrill`. North/East/South share 64° downward orthographic cameras, scale 6.75 and zero ground target. Use drawSize (6.75,6.75) and drawOffset (0,0,0) for one unit per game tile. Final 512px transparent color/mask pairs have 12px black unselected outlines. Saved-scene checks and 512px/256px review sheets are generated.
